Calculate BMI, body mass index


What is BMI?

BMI (Body Mass Index) is a measurement that uses your height and weight to estimate whether your weight is within a healthy range.


How is BMI calculated?

BMI is calculated by dividing your weight in kilograms by the square of your height in meters.

Formula: BMI = Weight (kg) / Height² (m²)


BMI Categories

Underweight: Below 18.5

Healthy weight: 18.5 – 24.9

Overweight: 25 – 29.9

Obesity: 30 and above


Important Note

BMI is a general screening tool, not a complete measure of health. It does not directly measure body fat and may not accurately reflect everyone's health. For children and teenagers, BMI is interpreted differently based on age and sex.
